Event overview

About the Event

SEU TechConnect 2026 is a demonstration event created for this HTML lab assignment. The page shows how a university technology event can present useful information in a clear and structured format.

The main purpose is to bring students together around web development, artificial intelligence, robotics, and practical problem solving. Participants can attend sessions, view student projects, and register their area of interest.

Participation Details

Eligibility: Current Southeast University students from any department.

Venue: Department of Computer Science and Engineering, Southeast University.

Participation: Registration is free for this classroom demonstration page.

Visit the Southeast University website

Why the event matters

Event Goals

Core Goals

  • Encourage students to share small technology projects and practical ideas.
  • Introduce current areas such as web development, AI, data analysis, IoT, and robotics.
  • Build confidence through short presentations, teamwork, and peer discussion.
  • Create a friendly space for students from different semesters to learn together.

What a Participant Can Gain

Students can observe project demonstrations, discuss tools with peers, and identify topics they may want to explore in later courses or personal projects.

This page is an academic lab project and not an official event announcement.

How to Participate
